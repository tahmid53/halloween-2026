# Halloween Night

Party games for Saturday, October 31: Secret Assassin missions and costume contest voting.
It runs in any phone browser. Guests don't need an account or an app.

**Live site:** https://tahmid53.github.io/halloween-2026/

## Host checklist

1. **Before the party:** open the site on your phone, go to **Host tools**, tap **Set up the party**, and paste your guest list.
2. **Once most guests have arrived:** tap **Deal missions**. Tap **Show code** next to each guest and let them scan it with their phone camera, or tap **Copy their link** and text it to them.
3. **Back it up:** open **Backup**, tap **Copy backup**, and paste it into Notes. Everything the host needs lives on this one phone.
4. **Costume contest:** after the parade, tap **Open voting** and show the voting code (or copy the voting link and text the group). As guests finish, tap **Scan ballots** and scan each ballot code. Then tap **Reveal the winners**.

Late arrivals: add them in **Guests** so they can vote. To join Secret Assassin, any player who's still in taps **Bring in a late arrival** on their phone and the newcomer scans that code.

## How it works

There's no server. Missions, hand-offs and ballots travel between phones as QR links, and each phone keeps its own state in the browser, so everyone should stick to the same phone and browser all night.

- **Missions:** the targets form one shuffled loop. When someone is assassinated, they show a hand-over code and their assassin scans it to inherit their target and weapon.
- **Ballots:** each guest's ballot carries their personal code from their mission link, so the host's phone can tell real ballots from made-up ones. Nobody can vote for themselves.
- **Awards:** with "One award per person" on, Best Overall is decided first, and anyone who tops two categories keeps the one with more votes.
